FAQ

UPSC Prelims practice, answered

How Abhyaas works, what the predicted score means, and whether you need an account to start.

What is Abhyaas?

Abhyaas is a free UPSC Civil Services Prelims practice app. You take a short diagnostic quiz, then get a statistical predicted Prelims score with a confidence band that narrows as you practice more.

How does UPSC Prelims score prediction work?

After you answer the diagnostic questions, Abhyaas estimates your expected Prelims score as a mean with lower and upper bounds. Confidence starts lower and rises as you complete more focused practice on weak topics.

Do I need to sign up to start the free test?

No. You can start the free diagnostic without registration. Sign in later with Google if you want to save progress, sync across devices, and continue targeted practice.

What kinds of questions does Abhyaas use?

Practice mixes real previous-year questions (PYQs) with AI-generated, exam-level items tagged by subject and topic so you can drill weak areas after your predicted band appears.

Is Abhyaas only for serious UPSC aspirants?

No. It helps both beginners who want a realistic baseline on day one and active aspirants who want a clearer readiness signal than generic mock scores alone.
