Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 August 2026

Vana Intellikrafts Solutions Private Limited (“Vana”, “we”, “us”) operates vanaintellikrafts.in and builds Abhyaas, an EdTech platform for competitive exam preparation. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our website or use our products.

Information we collect

  • Website visitors: standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages viewed, timestamps) collected by our hosting provider for security and performance.
  • Abhyaas users: account details you provide (such as name and email), quiz responses, performance metrics, and usage data needed to deliver score modeling and practice features. Abhyaas may have additional product-specific terms.
  • Contact: information you send when emailing founder@vanaintellikrafts.in.

How we use information

We use collected information to operate and improve our services, provide statistical readiness features, respond to inquiries, maintain security, and comply with applicable law. We do not sell personal data.

Legal basis and consent

Processing is based on your consent where required, contractual necessity to provide services you request, and our legitimate interests in operating and improving our platforms, subject to applicable Indian law including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, as implemented.

Sharing

We may share data with infrastructure providers (hosting, email, analytics) under contractual safeguards, or when required by law. We do not share data with third parties for their independent marketing.

Retention

We retain information only as long as needed for the purposes described or as required by law, after which it is deleted or anonymized.

Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the nature of the data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data, or withdraw consent where processing is consent-based, by contacting us at the email below.

Children

Our services are intended for exam aspirants generally aged 18 and above. If you believe we have collected data from a minor without appropriate consent, please contact us.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
